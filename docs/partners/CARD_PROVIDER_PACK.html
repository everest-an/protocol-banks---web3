2. Security overview

This is a factual overview, not a certification. No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or PCI DSS attestation is claimed; the issuer remains responsible for cardholder data and BIN sponsorship.

Architecture

Key management

  • Wallet auth: wallet signature, email magic link or OAuth; the wallet address is the account identifier.
  • Custom non-custodial wallet: Shamir 2-of-3 shares (device, server, recovery code); the server cannot reconstruct the key alone.
  • PIN-derived encryption: PBKDF2 (100k iterations) + AES-256-GCM. Private keys are never logged.
  • Trading: Hyperliquid approveAgent with trading-only rights; cannot withdraw.

Agent API and payments

  • API keys: 256-bit random secret, shown once, stored as SHA-256 hash with a short prefix.
  • Policy: allowedOps, maxNotionalUsd, dailyCapUsd, allowedCoins; a key cannot widen its own policy; rate limits and optional IP/origin allowlists.
  • EIP-3009 payments: signer must be the key owner; recipient, amount, chain/token and validity window are checked; on-chain nonce prevents replay.
  • The relayer submits the payer's signed authorization and pays gas; the platform never holds the funds.

Monitoring, incident response, limitations

  • Audit events for keys, policy changes, orders, payments and card actions; daily card reconciliation.
  • Incident response: detect → contain (disable keys, freeze cards) → assess → notify → remediate.
  • Known limitations: no third-party certification yet; relayer nonce collision across serverless instances is being hardened; single trading venue; provider holds card funds; no insurance.

3. AML / KYC policy

Draft for issuer and counsel review. The card issuer/partner is the regulated entity that performs cardholder KYC and owns the AML program; this describes the controls Protocol Bank operates on top.

Scope and regulatory frame

  • Hong Kong AMLO/CTF (Cap. 615), FATF recommendations, applicable sanctions lists, PDPO (Cap. 486).
  • Where jurisdictions differ, the stricter requirement applies. This is not legal advice.

Roles

RoleResponsibility
Card issuer / partnerCardholder KYC/KYB, sanctions/PEP screening, BIN sponsorship, card balance custody, issuer monitoring, SAR/STR filing
Protocol Bank compliance owner (to be named)Onboarding gate, limits, agent/card monitoring, escalation, recordkeeping, annual review
Engineering / supportServer-side caps, freeze controls, audit logs, secure key handling, complaints and escalation

Customer due diligence

  • Platform account: wallet signature / magic link / OAuth; wallet is the account identifier; a card is requested per agent.
  • Card applicant: provider-run identity verification (ID, liveness where required, address, DOB, nationality) or KYB (legal name, registration, address, directors/UBOs, ownership, source of funds).
  • Risk rating low/medium/high by the issuer; high-risk applicants require EDD and may be declined.
  • No anonymous or unhosted cards; no sanctioned jurisdictions; no prohibited businesses.

Monitoring and limits

Suspected money laundering, sanctions hits or fraud are escalated to the issuer's MLRO; the issuer files SARs under its own procedures.

Recordkeeping, training, review

  • KYC records held by the issuer; platform keeps account/agent/card/limit/transaction records for at least five years.
  • Annual AML/CTF training; annual policy review and after incidents or new countries.
  • Independent review of platform controls before production and every two years.

4. Card program flow

A. Treasury-funded (Yativo)

Owner deposits USDC to the provider; provider credits a USD balance; each agent card has its own limit; the provider authorizes against the provider-held balance; the platform enforces per-agent policy and reconciles webhooks.

B. Per-user deposit (Rain)

Provider runs KYC per cardholder and issues a per-user deposit contract/address; the user/agent funds their own USDC; the card draws from that user's balance; browser-side PAN/CVC decryption; the platform stores no PAN/CVC.

Sequence

#StepControl
1Agent requests a cardAPI key policy and account status checked before the provider call
2Provider KYC/KYBIssuer approves or declines; no card before approval
3Card issuedPer-card limit, freeze/close controls, metadata returned
4FundingUSDC to provider balance (treasury or per-user deposit); webhook confirms

Open questions for the provider

  • KYC ownership and supported jurisdictions; which entity contracts and which MLRO owns SAR filing.
  • Deposit model (per-user contract vs treasury), supported chains, settlement currency.
  • Card limits, velocity, webhook catalogue/signature/retry, freeze/close latency.
  • PAN/CVC handling and what the platform is allowed to store.
  • Fees, minimum volume, production timeline after KYB.
