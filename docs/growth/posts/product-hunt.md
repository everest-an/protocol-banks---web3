people don't have — and "AI trading bots" are usually black boxes that
take your money.

What it does:
- Connect MetaMask, fund a trading budget on Hyperliquid
- Approve a trading-only agent wallet (EIP-712)
- The AI trades momentum + funding-carry signals 24/7
- Every trade is explained in plain language
- The agent can NEVER withdraw — worst case is the budget you chose

Paper mode runs on real market data with simulated money — try it with
zero risk at protocolbanks.com

Ask me anything about the strategy, the risk controls, or the agent
architecture (SIWE auth, AES-256-GCM key custody, per-user isolation).
